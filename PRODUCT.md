## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed on GitHub Pages at stanleyho.me (CNAME). The rebuild drops the legacy jQuery / Bootstrap / Isotope / template asset bundle where it is no longer used.

## What it is

Stanley Ho's personal site: an introduction plus portfolio. Stanley is an early-stage startup operator: a serial employee #1 / founding hire across pre-seed to Series A companies, now a founder in stealth, and also a designer on the side.

## Audience and job

- **Founders hiring for early roles** (seed to Series A): deciding whether Stanley is the operator / founding-product hire they want. Success means they reach out.
- **Investors and co-founder prospects** for the stealth company: judging his credibility as a founder.
- **Recruiters and general professional visitors**: a richer LinkedIn.

The primary action is contact: email (stanley_ho@outlook.com) or LinkedIn (linkedin.com/in/stanleyyenho).

## Positioning

Not a generalist PM. He joins at near-zero and turns it into revenue, product and team: Vitalize (employee #5, 7-figure ARR in under a year, $31M Series A), Samaritan (employee #9, automated 75% of CS workflows, raised 6-figure philanthropic funding, Series A), Atmos.ai (employee #1, built the $2M seed deck, first sale, v1 product; acquired by WAP Sustainability, then SLR Consulting). He also designs: Ivey Business Review covers, UX for a musician-follower app, social-enterprise media, decks, and the Mandate of Heaven clothing brand.

## Voice

Professional first (the user reversed an earlier "lean into personality" answer on 2026-10-10). The homepage reads as a credible operator and founder: clear, confident and specific. Personality is a light touch. The candid, funny long-form voice (including occasional profanity) stays in his own words on the About page but is not the headline. Side projects (Mandate of Heaven, Apolyon Product Lab / Apolyon Studios, the "Your Friend Stan" blog) are secondary content.

## Pages

- `index.html`: intro, work history (operations and product), a curated swipeable design reel (7 of the 23 pieces) and elsewhere. Email and LinkedIn sit in the intro.
- `about.html`: long-form bio, background, how he works, values, life outside work.
- No contact page or section: the user removed it (2026-10-10). Email (with copy) and LinkedIn live in the homepage intro and on About.

## Constraints and assets

- Real facts and numbers in the existing copy must be kept exactly. Do not invent metrics, clients or testimonials.
- Assets: headshot (`assets/img/about/headshot.jpg`), portfolio images in `assets/img/portfolio/`, and three Google Drive deck links.
- The 何 (Ho) character is the personal mark in the current nav. Treat it as a candidate brand commitment, not a confirmed one.
- Google Analytics (UA-158662780-1) is present.
- Accessibility: WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-navigable lightbox and nav, and support for reduced motion.

## Brand commitments

- Standing preference, confirmed by the user on 2026-10-10: the conventional, clean single-column personal site, executed at full craft. Benchmarks are paco.me (primary; precise small type, gutter-labelled sections, quiet motion) and leerob.com (the plain, fast, text-first spirit only, not its layout or positioning). The user asked for "some unique touch so it's a bit different from every other operator site". Expressive or themed worlds were reviewed and rejected as not professional enough.
